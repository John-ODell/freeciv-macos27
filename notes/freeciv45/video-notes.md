## 5. Feel

- **Pace overall:** 10 turns in 14:55 is about 0.7 turns per minute, or roughly 90 seconds a turn (D).
- **Pace by half:** the first 6 minutes cover about 1 turn of big decisions: civics, Foreign Advisor, a trade and city reviews. D: turn 136 at [00:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=20s) and turn 137 at [06:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=376s). The rest runs at about 1 turn per minute (D: turn 146 at [14:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=890s)).
- **A typical turn:** he reads the log, handles one or two popups, gives orders to a few workers, makes one city decision, and sometimes opens diplomacy.
- **Map scale:** he stays at one zoom that shows roughly 15 to 20 tiles across (I). A typical frame holds 3 to 6 city banners and many units. O [04:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=290s), [09:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=545s). The grid is square. The HUD takes the bottom fifth of the screen and a slim top strip, and the score list overlays the map. O [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=70s)
- **Density:** cities are about 3 to 4 tiles apart (I), and their work areas overlap, which he weighs when settling. O [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=761s). Several workers serve each city, and military stacks of 3 or more sit on the borders. O [02:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=175s), [07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=440s)
- **What keeps him engaged:**
  - near-term goals with visible countdowns: GP odds, culture levels, growth and build turns, anarchy;
  - AI leaders who visibly remember things, through the attitude reasons and a demand;
  - a tech market with a constant stream of tradeable items;
  - races to wonders and religions that he can lose to someone else, as he does at [09:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xC5RXhifRy8&t=562s);
  - numbers in every tooltip, which make each small choice feel informed;
  - planning the next war out loud.

## 6. Takeaways for civ45, in priority order

1. **Numbers on hover, everywhere.** Tiles, units and cities should have hover summaries, and every order should preview its yield change, side effects and turns. The Qt client today has only a middle-click tile popup and no map tooltips (checked). This is the biggest gap in feel.
2. **Pre-attack odds in the hover.** Show win odds, the likely HP left, the XP at stake and a modifier list. Freeciv already computes the win chance (checked `common/clientutils.c`), so this is client work. Also decide whether a lost defender should take its whole stack with it: Freeciv's `killstack` defaults to on (checked `server/settings.c`), and Civ IV kills only the defender (I).
3. **The city screen as the hub, plus fast paths around it.** Keep the food, production and culture bars with turns and a clear starvation warning, specialists with GP output, the governor, and a production list with turns and tooltips. Add banners, a city hover summary, a build-complete chooser and a build bar from the map. Freeciv's `citydlg.cpp` already covers most of the core.
4. **A worker economy that rewards micro.** Improvements that grow over time (cottages), chops that turn into production, chance finds when mining, and civics that speed workers up. Keep an automation toggle and suggested tiles. Freeciv's `Activity_Time` effect only overrides build time, with no percentage form (checked `doc/README.effects`).
5. **Civics as parallel slots.** 5 categories, each civic with an upkeep level, a total cost and a short anarchy. Freeciv has one government plus policy multipliers (checked). Decide whether to model slots with multipliers or with engine work.
6. **Diplomacy with reasons.** An attitude word with its +/- memories, a who-will-trade-what tech grid, deals in resources, gold per turn and open borders, third-party war requests, and AI demands. Freeciv lacks these clause types and the AI behaviour (checked `diplodlg.cpp`, `daidiplomacy.c`).
7. **Chosen promotions.** XP should come from combat and from civics or buildings at build time, and spending it should be a visible button choice. Freeciv has only veteran levels (checked `Veteran_Build`).
8. **Great People.** Typed points from specialists and wonders, a top-bar progress readout with type odds, and announcements of rival births. Freeciv has nothing equivalent (checked effects list).
9. **Religion.** Founding through tech races, spread and missionaries, shrine income, a civic that reacts to religion, and an attitude penalty for a different religion. Freeciv has nothing equivalent (I, from the effects list).
10. **Culture-driven borders.** Each city should have a culture level with turns to the next, border growth when it levels up, and per-tile culture shares. Freeciv tracks culture (`History` and `Performance` effects), but border radius grows with city size, not culture (checked `common/borders.c`). A `Border_Strength_Pct` effect and a `MinCulture` requirement exist (checked `doc/README.effects`, `common/fc_types.h`). I: combined, they could let culture win contested tiles.

## 7. Open questions

- **Which interface to target (I):** the odds detail, trade alerts and clock seen here look like a mod rather than the stock game. Should civ45 match stock Beyond the Sword, or this richer modded interface?
- **Ruleset or engine (I from section 4):** items 6 to 10 in section 6, plus resource trading and civic slots, have no direct Freeciv 3.2 equivalent. How far can ruleset effects and Lua scripts approximate them, and which belong to the planned civ54 engine fork instead?
- **Whipping and buying:** neither is shown here. Freeciv has gold buying but no population-rush action (checked `common/actions.h`, which has no hurry or rush action). Another episode where the player runs Slavery is needed.
- **Health and espionage:** neither is exercised; health is a counter and espionage sits at 0%. Freeciv's illness model (`Health_Pct`) works differently from Civ IV's food-penalty health. Which model should civ45 use?
- **Early game:** this part starts at turn 136, so exploration, huts and the first cities need an earlier part.
- **Camera and grid:** Civ IV uses a tilted 3D camera on a square grid. Is civ45 aiming at a square or isometric 2D tileset, and at what default zoom?
- **City radius and pace (I):** check that the civ45 city radius matches Civ IV's 21-tile work area, and pick a turn count and calendar. This game has 500 turns at 20 years per turn in this era.
- **Governor:** the governor chose specialists for Great Person points here. How much of Freeciv's governor should civ45 expose, and should it weigh GP points?
